Getting started with WiFi or ISP

Everything you need for the first few minutes, and answers to the questions people ask most.

By Corey Haines, maker of WiFi or ISP · Updated October 6, 2026

Install

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Download the DMG, open it, and drag WiFi or ISP to Applications. Or, with Homebrew: brew install --cask coreyhaines31/tap/wifiorisp. It's signed and notarized by Apple, and updates itself.

Your first two minutes

  1. Open it from Applications. There's no Dock icon or main window. It lives in the menu bar, as a small router icon near the clock.
  2. Answer the welcome window. Allow Location access if you want to see your Wi-Fi network's name (macOS treats it as location data; the app never reads your location). Leave “Open at login” on so it keeps watching.
  3. Allow local network access. macOS 15 and later ask once; the app needs it to time your router.
  4. Allow notifications if you want them. They tell you about drops and weak connections.
  5. Give it 30 seconds. The first verdict needs a few measurements.

Can't see the icon? On MacBooks with a notch, a full menu bar hides icons behind it. Hold ⌘ and drag other icons aside, or quit a few menu bar apps.

Reading the menu

The router icon's three lights show your Wi-Fi signal. When something is slow, the lights go dark and a word appears beside it: WiFi, Router, or ISP. A slash means you're not connected to any network.

Troubleshooting

It says “Unknown network”

Location access isn't allowed. Click Show Network Name… in the menu, or turn on WiFi or ISP in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

The router “doesn't answer probes”

Some routers ignore the probes; the verdict then relies on your signal and the internet's timing. If that's new, check System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and make sure WiFi or ISP is on.

It says “Wired”

Your Mac is using Ethernet, so there's no Wi-Fi signal to show. Your router and the internet are still measured.

I'm on a VPN

It still times the router of the Wi-Fi network underneath. It skips identifying your provider, which would name the VPN.

A speed or lag test failed

Both need a working internet connection. If it's down, the verdict will say so.

Privacy

No analytics and no account. History stays on your Mac. In the background, the app times a handshake to your router and to two internet addresses every few seconds (no data is sent in them), and asks Cloudflare once per network which provider you're on. Speed and lag tests run only when you start them. The full method.

Uninstall

Quit it from its menu and drag it to the Trash. If it opens at login, turn that off first in its Settings, or in System Settings → General → Login Items. With Homebrew, brew uninstall --zap --cask wifiorisp also removes its history and settings.
