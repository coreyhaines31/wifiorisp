WiFi Explorer alternative

WiFi Explorer scans the air.
WiFi or ISP follows your connection.

WiFi Explorer is one of the best tools for seeing every Wi-Fi network around you: channels, widths, overlaps, and configuration problems. It looks at the radio environment. It doesn't look at your internet.

By Corey Haines, maker of WiFi or ISP · Updated October 6, 2026

The short answer

Picking a channel or debugging an access point's configuration? WiFi Explorer. Figuring out why your internet gets slow, and whether it's your Wi-Fi or your ISP? WiFi or ISP, which is free and runs in the background.

What WiFi Explorer does better

Seeing the airwaves. It lists every nearby network with its channel, band, width, security, and vendor, graphs the spectrum so you can spot overlap, decodes the details access points broadcast, and flags configuration issues. When the answer to slow Wi-Fi is “move to a quieter channel,” WiFi Explorer shows you which one.

What WiFi or ISP does better

Your connection, end to end, over time. It times your router and the internet together to say which side is slow, logs drops and slowdowns in the background, alerts you when they happen, and tests speed and bufferbloat. If WiFi or ISP says “WiFi or router,” WiFi Explorer is a great next step for finding a better channel.

Questions

Is WiFi Explorer free?

No. WiFi Explorer is $19.99 with a short free trial, and WiFi Explorer Pro 3 is $129.99 direct. WiFi or ISP is free.

Do they work together?

Yes. Use WiFi or ISP to find out whether the problem is on your side, and WiFi Explorer to pick a less crowded channel if it is.

Find out which side is slow.

WiFi or ISP is a free Mac menu bar app. It times your router and the internet at the same moment, says which side is slow, and keeps a log you can send your ISP.
