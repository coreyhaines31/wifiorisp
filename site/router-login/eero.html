Router login

eero router login

How to get into your eero settings, and the three settings that fix most slow Wi-Fi: the channel, a restart, and smart queueing.

By Corey Haines, maker of WiFi or ISP · Updated October 6, 2026

How to log in

eero has no web admin page. You manage it in the eero app.

eero home networks are managed only in the eero app; there is no browser admin page. eero's support says the network can't be configured through a web browser or on Windows, Linux or Intel-based Macs. (eero Insight at insight.eero.com is for eero Business only.)

Username and password

No router password. You sign in to the eero app with a one-time code sent by text or email, or with a linked Amazon account.

Change the Wi-Fi channel

  1. Not user-changeable. eero uses automatic channel selection and Dynamic Frequency Selection to move to less congested channels; eero suggests a soft reset so the system re-learns your environment.

Not sure which channel? Here's how to pick a quiet one.

Restart it

  1. eero app: Settings > Advanced networking > more options (…) > Restart Network, then confirm.
  2. Or unplug each eero briefly and plug it back in.

Smart queueing (fixes lag under load)

Optimize for Conferencing and Gaming (formerly Smart Queue Management / SQM). Shapes queues to cut latency on busy networks. eero's help center has described it under Settings > Advanced networking > SQM; newer app versions list it under Discover > eero Labs (path varies by app version). eero advises enabling it only if your measured speeds match your plan. Model support varies.

Test for bufferbloat before and after turning it on.

Firmware updates

Automatic. Updates install during your Preferred update time (eero app: Settings > Software updates > Preferred update time). The network reboots briefly during updates.

Find your router's address on a Mac

  1. Open Apple menu > System Settings, then click Wi-Fi in the sidebar.
  2. Click Details next to the network you're connected to.
  3. Click TCP/IP. The address next to Router is your router's address.
  4. Shortcut: Option-click the Wi-Fi icon in the menu bar; the router address is listed under your network.

See if your router is the slow part.

WiFi or ISP is a free Mac menu bar app. It times your router and the internet at the same moment, says which side is slow, and keeps a log you can send your ISP.
