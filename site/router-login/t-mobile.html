Router login

T-Mobile router login

How to get into your T-Mobile 5G Home Internet Gateway settings, and the three settings that fix most slow Wi-Fi: the channel, a restart, and smart queueing.

By Corey Haines, maker of WiFi or ISP · Updated October 6, 2026

How to log in

  1. Connect to its network over Wi-Fi or a cable.
  2. Open a browser and go to 192.168.12.1.
  3. Sign in with the admin password (see below).

T-Mobile now puts gateway management in the T-Life app (formerly the T-Mobile Internet app). Many gateway models also have a web interface at http://192.168.12.1; what it lets you change varies by model (Nokia, Arcadyan, Sagemcom).

Username and password

T-Mobile says the default admin username and password are on the label on the underside of the gateway. For some older devices T-Mobile notes the default password matches the Wi-Fi password on that label.

Restart it

  1. App: on the Overview screen, confirm the gateway is online and select Restart your gateway.
  2. Web interface (models that have one): System tab > Reboot, then confirm.
  3. Or turn it off and on with the power button / power cable.

Find your router's address on a Mac

  1. Open Apple menu > System Settings, then click Wi-Fi in the sidebar.
  2. Click Details next to the network you're connected to.
  3. Click TCP/IP. The address next to Router is your router's address.
  4. Shortcut: Option-click the Wi-Fi icon in the menu bar; the router address is listed under your network.

See if your router is the slow part.

WiFi or ISP is a free Mac menu bar app. It times your router and the internet at the same moment, says which side is slow, and keeps a log you can send your ISP.
