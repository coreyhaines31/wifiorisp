Router login

ASUS router login

How to get into your ASUS settings, and the three settings that fix most slow Wi-Fi: the channel, a restart, and smart queueing.

By Corey Haines, maker of WiFi or ISP · Updated October 6, 2026

How to log in

  1. Connect to its network over Wi-Fi or a cable.
  2. Open a browser and go to www.asusrouter.com, 192.168.50.1.
  3. Sign in with the admin password (see below).

Web GUI at http://www.asusrouter.com or the router's LAN IP (ASUS's example is 192.168.50.1; it varies by model). The ASUS Router app is an alternative. ASUS's Device Discovery Utility finds the IP if you don't know it.

Username and password

ASUS says some models have a default login printed on a sticker on the back or bottom; other models show model-specific credentials on that label. This login is separate from the Wi-Fi name and password.

Change the Wi-Fi channel

  1. Sign in at www.asusrouter.com.
  2. Go to Wireless > General.
  3. Pick the band (2.4 / 5 / 6 GHz), set Control Channel, click Apply.

By default ASUS picks the control channel automatically. Available channels depend on your country.

Not sure which channel? Here's how to pick a quiet one.

Restart it

  1. Sign in to the web GUI and click Reboot, then OK.

Smart queueing (fixes lag under load)

Adaptive QoS. Adaptive QoS > QoS > Enable QoS, choose QoS type (Adaptive QoS, Traditional QoS, or Bandwidth Limiter; one at a time), pick a mode, Apply. Not every model supports it; ASUS has a separate FAQ to check yours.

Test for bufferbloat before and after turning it on.

Firmware updates

Advanced Settings > Administration > Firmware Upgrade (check, upload a file, or enable Auto Firmware Upgrade). ASUS Router app: Settings > Firmware Upgrade.

Find your router's address on a Mac

  1. Open Apple menu > System Settings, then click Wi-Fi in the sidebar.
  2. Click Details next to the network you're connected to.
  3. Click TCP/IP. The address next to Router is your router's address.
  4. Shortcut: Option-click the Wi-Fi icon in the menu bar; the router address is listed under your network.
