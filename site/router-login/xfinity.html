Router login

Xfinity router login

How to get into your Xfinity (Comcast) Gateway settings, and the three settings that fix most slow Wi-Fi: the channel, a restart, and smart queueing.

By Corey Haines, maker of WiFi or ISP · Updated October 6, 2026

How to log in

  1. Connect to its network over Wi-Fi or a cable.
  2. Open a browser and go to 10.0.0.1.
  3. Sign in with the admin password (see below).

Xfinity recommends the Xfinity app for most people. The Admin Tool at http://10.0.0.1 still exists, but you must be on your home network, and Xfinity says Admin Tool access has to be toggled on (from the Xfinity app) before 10.0.0.1 works. On XB6 and newer gateways, advanced Wi-Fi settings are not shown in the Admin Tool.

Username and password

Admin Tool username is admin. Xfinity says to check the sticker on the bottom of your Xfinity Gateway for the default password, if you haven't changed it.

Change the Wi-Fi channel

  1. XB2 and XB3 gateways only: go to http://10.0.0.1 and sign in.
  2. Go to Gateway > Connection > Wi-Fi.
  3. Click EDIT next to the band you want to change.
  4. Select Manual for Channel Selection, then pick a channel.
  5. Click Save Settings at the bottom of the page.

On XB6 and newer gateways, Xfinity says Wi-Fi settings are optimized automatically and advanced Wi-Fi settings cannot be managed from the Admin Tool; Xfinity points these customers to the Xfinity app instead.

Not sure which channel? Here's how to pick a quiet one.

Restart it

  1. Xfinity app: tap the WiFi icon, tap Troubleshoot, then Restart your Gateway.
  2. Or unplug the gateway's power cable, wait one minute, and plug it back in.
  3. Xfinity says a restart can take up to 12 minutes, during which your home network is down.

Firmware updates

Not user-managed in the docs we found. Xfinity rents and maintains the gateway; no manual firmware-update path is documented.

Find your router's address on a Mac

  1. Open Apple menu > System Settings, then click Wi-Fi in the sidebar.
  2. Click Details next to the network you're connected to.
  3. Click TCP/IP. The address next to Router is your router's address.
  4. Shortcut: Option-click the Wi-Fi icon in the menu bar; the router address is listed under your network.

See if your router is the slow part.

WiFi or ISP is a free Mac menu bar app. It times your router and the internet at the same moment, says which side is slow, and keeps a log you can send your ISP.
