Router login

AirPort router login

How to get into your Apple AirPort (discontinued) settings, and the three settings that fix most slow Wi-Fi: the channel, a restart, and smart queueing.

By Corey Haines, maker of WiFi or ISP · Updated October 6, 2026

How to log in

AirPort has no web admin page. You manage it in the AirPort Utility.

AirPort Extreme, AirPort Express and AirPort Time Capsule have no web admin page; they're configured with AirPort Utility (in Applications > Utilities on Mac, or the iOS app). Apple has discontinued all AirPort hardware and AirPort Utility: macOS 26, iOS 26 and iPadOS 26 are the last versions that officially support it, though upgrading to macOS 27 or iOS 27 doesn't remove an installed copy.

Username and password

AirPort Utility asks for the base station password you set when configuring it.

Change the Wi-Fi channel

  1. Open AirPort Utility, select the base station, click Edit.
  2. Click Wireless, then Wireless Options near the bottom.
  3. Choose channels from the 2.4 GHz Channel and 5 GHz Channel pop-up menus.
  4. Click Save, then Update.

Not sure which channel? Here's how to pick a quiet one.

Restart it

  1. AirPort Utility on Mac: Base Station > Restart.
  2. If that doesn't work, unplug it for a few seconds and plug it back in.

Smart queueing

No QoS or smart-queue feature is documented in AirPort Utility's help.

Firmware updates

Select the base station in AirPort Utility; an Update button appears if firmware is available.

Find your router's address on a Mac

  1. Open Apple menu > System Settings, then click Wi-Fi in the sidebar.
  2. Click Details next to the network you're connected to.
  3. Click TCP/IP. The address next to Router is your router's address.
  4. Shortcut: Option-click the Wi-Fi icon in the menu bar; the router address is listed under your network.

See if your router is the slow part.

WiFi or ISP is a free Mac menu bar app. It times your router and the internet at the same moment, says which side is slow, and keeps a log you can send your ISP.
