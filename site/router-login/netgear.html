Router login

NETGEAR router login

How to get into your NETGEAR (Nighthawk and Orbi) settings, and the three settings that fix most slow Wi-Fi: the channel, a restart, and smart queueing.

By Corey Haines, maker of WiFi or ISP · Updated October 6, 2026

How to log in

  1. Connect to its network over Wi-Fi or a cable.
  2. Open a browser and go to routerlogin.net, 192.168.1.1, orbilogin.com.
  3. Sign in with the admin password (see below).

Nighthawk routers: routerlogin.net or http://192.168.1.1. Orbi: orbilogin.com. The Nighthawk app and Orbi app are alternatives (not every model supports the app).

Username and password

Username admin. NETGEAR says the password is the one you set during setup; newer routers make you change the default during setup. NETGEAR also says to check the label on the bottom or back of the router for your model's login details. Password recovery is available if you enabled it.

Change the Wi-Fi channel

  1. Sign in at routerlogin.net (Orbi: orbilogin.com).
  2. Select Wireless (on some models ADVANCED > Wireless Settings).
  3. Scroll to the band you want and choose a number from the Channel menu.
  4. Click Apply. If you use extenders, set them to match.

Not every channel is available in every region.

Not sure which channel? Here's how to pick a quiet one.

Restart it

  1. Sign in, then click ADVANCED > Reboot (Nighthawk Pro Gaming: three-dot menu > Reboot).
  2. Settings are kept on reboot.

Smart queueing (fixes lag under load)

Dynamic QoS (select Nighthawk models). Off by default. Sign in, select Dynamic QoS, tick Enable Dynamic QoS, enter your internet bandwidth, Apply. NETGEAR lists supported models (RAX series, R7000-class, XR1000 gaming routers, some mesh kits).

Test for bufferbloat before and after turning it on.

Firmware updates

ADVANCED > Administration (or Settings > Administration) > Firmware Update or Router Update > Check. Don't power off until the router restarts. Orbi has its own orbilogin.com upgrade article.

Find your router's address on a Mac

  1. Open Apple menu > System Settings, then click Wi-Fi in the sidebar.
  2. Click Details next to the network you're connected to.
  3. Click TCP/IP. The address next to Router is your router's address.
  4. Shortcut: Option-click the Wi-Fi icon in the menu bar; the router address is listed under your network.
