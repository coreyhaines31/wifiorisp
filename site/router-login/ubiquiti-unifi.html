Router login

UniFi router login

How to get into your Ubiquiti UniFi (Dream Router, UniFi Express) settings, and the three settings that fix most slow Wi-Fi: the channel, a restart, and smart queueing.

By Corey Haines, maker of WiFi or ISP · Updated October 6, 2026

How to log in

  1. Connect to its network over Wi-Fi or a cable.
  2. Open a browser and go to unifi, 192.168.1.1.
  3. Sign in with the admin password (see below).

Setup is done in the UniFi mobile app. Afterwards you can manage it in the app, at unifi.ui.com with remote management, or locally: Ubiquiti says to open a browser on the local network and enter the console's IP address, or type 'unifi/' if a UniFi gateway is on the network.

Username and password

You sign in with your UI Account (or, for local-only setups, the local admin account you created during setup). No password is printed on the device.

Change the Wi-Fi channel

  1. In UniFi Network: set channels for all APs under Radios, or per access point in UniFi Devices > select the AP > Settings.
  2. Ubiquiti recommends only channels 1, 6 or 11 on 2.4 GHz.

Menu names come from Ubiquiti's help center and shift between UniFi Network versions.

Smart queueing (fixes lag under load)

Smart Queues. Built to prevent bufferbloat on slower connections. Ubiquiti does not recommend it at 300 Mbps or above and says it can reduce throughput by up to 30%. Needs a UniFi gateway.

Test for bufferbloat before and after turning it on.

Firmware updates

Settings > Control Plane > Updates (automatic updates can be scheduled there).

Find your router's address on a Mac

  1. Open Apple menu > System Settings, then click Wi-Fi in the sidebar.
  2. Click Details next to the network you're connected to.
  3. Click TCP/IP. The address next to Router is your router's address.
  4. Shortcut: Option-click the Wi-Fi icon in the menu bar; the router address is listed under your network.

See if your router is the slow part.

WiFi or ISP is a free Mac menu bar app. It times your router and the internet at the same moment, says which side is slow, and keeps a log you can send your ISP.
