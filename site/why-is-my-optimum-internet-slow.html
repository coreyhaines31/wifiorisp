Why is my Optimum internet slow?

Slow Optimum internet has two possible sources: your home Wi-Fi, or Optimum's network. Here's how to tell them apart, the slowdowns typical of Optimum, and the fixes that work.

By Corey Haines, maker of WiFi or ISP · Updated October 6, 2026

The short answer

Compare your router with the internet. If the router is quick and the internet is slow, it's Optimum. Check for an outage, restart your equipment in the right order, and if it keeps happening, log it and contact Optimum with the evidence.

Optimum says it has no data caps (fiber plan page; Optimum vs. Xfinity comparison). Altice USA changed its corporate name to Optimum Communications, Inc. effective Nov 7, 2025, and its NYSE ticker from ATUS to OPTU. Where Fiber is not available, service runs over cable connected to Optimum's fiber network (Optimum FAQ). Support phone per Optimum's support article: 877.694.9474.

First, rule out your Wi-Fi

A slow Optimum connection and a slow Wi-Fi network feel identical. Time a round trip to your router and one to the internet at the same moment. If your router answers quickly and the internet doesn't, the slowdown is on Optimum's side. If even your router is slow, it's your Wi-Fi or router, and Optimum can't fix it. Here's how to check on a Mac.

What usually slows down Optimum

Optimum delivers internet over cable (HFC / DOCSIS), fiber (FTTH). The usual causes:

  • Wi-Fi rather than wired: Optimum says testing over Wi-Fi will almost always show slower speeds than a wired Ethernet connection.
  • Distance and obstructions between device and Gateway, and interference from baby monitors, cordless phones, Bluetooth devices or wireless cameras (Optimum WiFi tips).
  • Too many devices sharing bandwidth, plus background apps and automatic updates (Optimum articles).
  • Latency, jitter and packet loss can make the connection feel slow even when a speed test looks fine (Optimum article).
  • Outdated operating systems, drivers or firmware, and older devices that cannot handle faster speeds (Optimum WiFi tips).
  • A customer-owned modem that cannot handle the plan speed. Optimum requires DOCSIS 3.1, and after a speed upgrade you may need to reset the modem or set up new equipment.
  • An aging gateway: Optimum suggests upgrading equipment if your gateway is several years old.
  • VPNs, which Optimum says can cut speeds by 30-50%.

Upload versus download

Cable (HFC) plans are asymmetric: Optimum's own comparison lists cable upload speeds of 5 Mbps to 35 Mbps, even on plans with much faster downloads. Fiber plans are symmetric, up to 8 Gbps download / 8 Gbps upload on the 8 Gig plan. When an upload fills that smaller pipe, everything else lags, which is called bufferbloat. Test for it.

Check for a Optimum outage

Optimum outage page

Requires sign-in: Optimum says to go to optimum.net, sign in with your Optimum ID and password, then under Support (upper right) select Service status / Check for outages. Optimum's FAQ also points to an outage map.

Restart your Optimum equipment

  1. In the My Optimum app: sign in with your Optimum ID, tap WiFi > More options > Manage router settings, then tap Restart my Internet equipment. The restart takes about 5 minutes.
  2. Or chat with Allie, Optimum's 24/7 virtual assistant, to have your equipment restarted remotely (about 5 minutes).
  3. Manually: unplug the power cord from your Gateway or modem (remove the battery backup if you have one, and unplug your router if you have one).
  4. Wait 30 seconds.
  5. Plug the power cord back in (reinsert the battery backup and plug the router back in if applicable).
  6. Try to access the Internet as you normally do.
  7. With an Optimum One or Optimum TV box: Home > Settings > System > INTERNET/WIFI AND PHONE > REBOOT, or hold the diamond button on the box's front panel for 10 seconds until 'GW Reset' appears.

Your Optimum equipment

Optimum-provided Gateway (all-in-one modem and router). Install guides exist for 'Gateway 4' and 'Gateway 5' (the Gateway 4 guide label shows Ubee P/N UBC1322BA00). Fiber customers get a 'Fiber Gateway' (WiFi 6) that replaces the modem and router. Optimum One / Optimum TV boxes can also restart the gateway.

Admin page: Optimum documents web portals rather than a local IP: sign in at optimum.net/gateway (per Gateway 4/5 install guides) or router.optimum.net (Smart Router settings FAQ).

App: My Optimum app (WiFi > More options > Manage router settings; also WiFi > Troubleshoot my Internet)

Your own equipment: Yes for cable modems. Internet-only customers (no Optimum phone) can use their own DOCSIS-certified DOCSIS 3.1 modem or gateway; Optimum does not allow DOCSIS 2.0 or 3.0 modems. Customer-owned modems get no Optimum tech support or replacement, and you must call 877.694.9474 to activate one. Renting a modem costs 'as low as $15 per month' per the certified-modems page (Optimum's FAQ page says rentals start at $10/month). Optimum also says to make sure your own modem can handle your plan speed.

Before you call Optimum

Optimum recommends these steps:

  1. Check all cables and make sure your modem and router are powered (Optimum internet support article).
  2. Restart your Gateway/modem and router: unplug, wait 30 seconds, plug back in.
  3. Check for outages: sign in at optimum.net > Support > Service status, or look for service alerts in the app.
  4. Test on more than one device. If only one device is affected, toggle its Wi-Fi off and on, move it closer to the Gateway, or restart it.
  5. Use a wired Ethernet connection for demanding tasks, and for the most accurate speed test.
  6. Limit background apps and pause automatic updates, and disconnect devices you are not using.
  7. Put the router or Gateway in a central, open, elevated spot away from interference.
  8. Run the My Optimum app's WiFi > Troubleshoot my Internet flow.
  9. Contact support, who can do a remote system reset of your modem or Gateway.

When you do call, bring a record: when it was slow, for how long, and proof your router answered normally the whole time. That moves a ticket past “restart your router.”
