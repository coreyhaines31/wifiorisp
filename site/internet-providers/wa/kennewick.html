Upload speeds in Kennewick

Cable plans here top out at 1 Gbps up, against 6 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

5G home and fixed wireless

AT&T, Advanced High Speed Internet, Columbia iConnect, EV Holdings 1 LLC, PocketiNet, T-Mobile, Verizon, Ziply Fiber and xyTel offer wireless home internet in Kennewick. It needs no installer and is often cheaper, but speed depends on the signal where you live and on how busy the tower is in the evening.

Before you switch

If your internet is slow now, make sure the provider is the problem. The free WiFi or ISP Mac app times your router and the internet at the same moment and tells you which side is slow, and the bufferbloat test shows whether calls will stutter when the connection is busy.

Questions

What is the best internet provider in Kennewick, WA?

For most homes, fiber from Ziply Fiber if it reaches your street: up to 6 Gbps down and 6 Gbps up, with steady lag. It covers 50% of homes, so check your address first.

Is fiber internet available in Kennewick, WA?

Yes. Day Wireless, Spectrum, Ziply Fiber and xyTel report fiber in Kennewick, reaching at least 50% of homes.

What is the fastest internet in Kennewick, WA?

Ziply Fiber advertises up to 6 Gbps down and 6 Gbps up (fiber), offered to 50% of homes.

How many internet providers are in Kennewick, WA?

11: 4 wired (fiber, cable, or DSL) and 9 5G or fixed wireless, plus satellite from Starlink, Viasat, and HughesNet, which reaches almost every home.

Know whether it’s the provider before you switch.

WiFi or ISP is a free Mac menu bar app. It times your router and the internet at the same moment, says which side is slow, and keeps a log you can send your ISP.
