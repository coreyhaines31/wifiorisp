Dover has 6 home internet providers in the FCC's latest data, 3 of them wired. Fiber reaches at least 92% of homes, more than Delaware as a whole (58%), led by Verizon at up to 2.3 Gbps. Xfinity cable covers 100% of homes, with uploads up to 250 Mbps.

Upload speeds in Dover

Cable plans here top out at 250 Mbps up, against 2.3 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

5G home and fixed wireless

AT&T, Bloosurf LLC, T-Mobile and Verizon offer wireless home internet in Dover. It needs no installer and is often cheaper, but speed depends on the signal where you live and on how busy the tower is in the evening.

Before you switch

If your internet is slow now, make sure the provider is the problem. The free WiFi or ISP Mac app times your router and the internet at the same moment and tells you which side is slow, and the bufferbloat test shows whether calls will stutter when the connection is busy.

Questions

What is the best internet provider in Dover, DE?

For most homes, fiber from Verizon if it reaches your street: up to 2.3 Gbps down and 2.3 Gbps up, with steady lag. It covers 92% of homes, so check your address first.

Is fiber internet available in Dover, DE?

Yes. Verizon report fiber in Dover, reaching at least 92% of homes.

What is the fastest internet in Dover, DE?

Verizon advertises up to 2.3 Gbps down and 2.3 Gbps up (fiber), offered to 92% of homes.

How many internet providers are in Dover, DE?

6: 3 wired (fiber, cable, or DSL) and 4 5G or fixed wireless, plus satellite from Starlink, Viasat, and HughesNet, which reaches almost every home.

Know whether it’s the provider before you switch.
