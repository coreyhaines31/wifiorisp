Internet providers in Fort Myers, FL

Fort Myers has 10 home internet providers in the FCC's latest data, 7 of them wired. Fiber reaches at least 33% of homes, less than Florida as a whole (64%), led by Quantum Fiber at up to 8 Gbps. Xfinity cable covers 82% of homes, with uploads up to 250 Mbps.

Upload speeds in Fort Myers

Cable plans here top out at 250 Mbps up, against 40 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

5G home and fixed wireless

AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon offer wireless home internet in Fort Myers. It needs no installer and is often cheaper, but speed depends on the signal where you live and on how busy the tower is in the evening.

Before you switch

If your internet is slow now, make sure the provider is the problem. The free WiFi or ISP Mac app times your router and the internet at the same moment and tells you which side is slow, and the bufferbloat test shows whether calls will stutter when the connection is busy.

Questions

What is the best internet provider in Fort Myers, FL?

For most homes, fiber from Quantum Fiber if it reaches your street: up to 8 Gbps down and 8 Gbps up, with steady lag. It covers 33% of homes, so check your address first.

Is fiber internet available in Fort Myers, FL?

Yes. Blue Stream, CenturyLink, Hotwire Communications, Quantum Fiber, Summit Broadband Inc., Tillman Fiber and Xfinity report fiber in Fort Myers, reaching at least 33% of homes.

What is the fastest internet in Fort Myers, FL?

Quantum Fiber advertises up to 8 Gbps down and 8 Gbps up (fiber), offered to 33% of homes.

How many internet providers are in Fort Myers, FL?

10: 7 wired (fiber, cable, or DSL) and 3 5G or fixed wireless, plus satellite from Starlink, Viasat, and HughesNet, which reaches almost every home.

Know whether it’s the provider before you switch.

WiFi or ISP is a free Mac menu bar app. It times your router and the internet at the same moment, says which side is slow, and keeps a log you can send your ISP.
