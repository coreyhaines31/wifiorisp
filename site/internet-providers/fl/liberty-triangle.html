Internet providers in Liberty Triangle, FL

Liberty Triangle has 12 home internet providers in the FCC's latest data, 9 of them wired. Fiber reaches at least 31% of homes, less than Florida as a whole (64%), led by Metronet Holdings at up to 5.12 Gbps. Zito Media cable covers 45% of homes, with uploads up to 50 Mbps.

Upload speeds in Liberty Triangle

Cable plans here top out at 1 Gbps up, against 8 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

5G home and fixed wireless

AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon offer wireless home internet in Liberty Triangle. It needs no installer and is often cheaper, but speed depends on the signal where you live and on how busy the tower is in the evening.

Before you switch

If your internet is slow now, make sure the provider is the problem. The free WiFi or ISP Mac app times your router and the internet at the same moment and tells you which side is slow, and the bufferbloat test shows whether calls will stutter when the connection is busy.

Questions

What is the best internet provider in Liberty Triangle, FL?

For most homes, fiber from Metronet Holdings if it reaches your street: up to 5.12 Gbps down and 5.12 Gbps up, with steady lag. It covers 31% of homes, so check your address first.

Is fiber internet available in Liberty Triangle, FL?

Yes. CenturyLink, Cox Communications, Hotwire Communications, Metronet Holdings, Quantum Fiber, Spectrum and Zito Media report fiber in Liberty Triangle, reaching at least 31% of homes.

What is the fastest internet in Liberty Triangle, FL?

Metronet Holdings advertises up to 5.12 Gbps down and 5.12 Gbps up (fiber), offered to 31% of homes.

How many internet providers are in Liberty Triangle, FL?

12: 9 wired (fiber, cable, or DSL) and 3 5G or fixed wireless, plus satellite from Starlink, Viasat, and HughesNet, which reaches almost every home.

Know whether it’s the provider before you switch.
