Internet providers in Daytona Beach, FL

Daytona Beach has 9 home internet providers in the FCC's latest data, 7 of them wired. Fiber reaches at least 46% of homes, less than Florida as a whole (64%), led by AT&T at up to 5 Gbps. Spectrum cable covers 85% of homes, with uploads up to 1 Gbps.

Upload speeds in Daytona Beach

Cable plans here top out at 1 Gbps up, against 10 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

5G home and fixed wireless

AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon offer wireless home internet in Daytona Beach. It needs no installer and is often cheaper, but speed depends on the signal where you live and on how busy the tower is in the evening.

Before you switch

If your internet is slow now, make sure the provider is the problem. The free WiFi or ISP Mac app times your router and the internet at the same moment and tells you which side is slow, and the bufferbloat test shows whether calls will stutter when the connection is busy.

Questions

What is the best internet provider in Daytona Beach, FL?

For most homes, fiber from AT&T if it reaches your street: up to 5 Gbps down and 5 Gbps up, with steady lag. It covers 46% of homes, so check your address first.

Is fiber internet available in Daytona Beach, FL?

Yes. AT&T, ClearWave, Hotwire Communications, Metronet Holdings, Spectrum, Velofi and Wire 3 report fiber in Daytona Beach, reaching at least 46% of homes.

What is the fastest internet in Daytona Beach, FL?

Wire 3 advertises up to 10 Gbps down and 10 Gbps up (fiber), offered to 29% of homes.

How many internet providers are in Daytona Beach, FL?

9: 7 wired (fiber, cable, or DSL) and 3 5G or fixed wireless, plus satellite from Starlink, Viasat, and HughesNet, which reaches almost every home.

Know whether it’s the provider before you switch.

WiFi or ISP is a free Mac menu bar app. It times your router and the internet at the same moment, says which side is slow, and keeps a log you can send your ISP.
