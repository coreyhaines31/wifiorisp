Upload speeds in Christiansburg

Cable plans here top out at 250 Mbps up, against 10 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

5G home and fixed wireless

AT&T, All Points Broadband, T-Mobile and Verizon offer wireless home internet in Christiansburg. It needs no installer and is often cheaper, but speed depends on the signal where you live and on how busy the tower is in the evening.

Before you switch

If your internet is slow now, make sure the provider is the problem. The free WiFi or ISP Mac app times your router and the internet at the same moment and tells you which side is slow, and the bufferbloat test shows whether calls will stutter when the connection is busy.

What is the best internet provider in Christiansburg, VA?

Is fiber internet available in Christiansburg, VA?

Barely: Citizens Telephone Cooperative, GigaBeam Networks, LLC and Shentel report fiber to 3% of homes in Christiansburg. Check your address to see if yours is one.

What is the fastest internet in Christiansburg, VA?

How many internet providers are in Christiansburg, VA?

9: 5 wired (fiber, cable, or DSL) and 4 5G or fixed wireless, plus satellite from Starlink, Viasat, and HughesNet, which reaches almost every home.
