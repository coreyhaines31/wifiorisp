Upload speeds in Lake Oswego

Cable plans here top out at 250 Mbps up, against 6 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

5G home and fixed wireless

AT&T, Elevate Technology Group, T-Mobile and Verizon offer wireless home internet in Lake Oswego. It needs no installer and is often cheaper, but speed depends on the signal where you live and on how busy the tower is in the evening.

Before you switch

If your internet is slow now, make sure the provider is the problem. The free WiFi or ISP Mac app times your router and the internet at the same moment and tells you which side is slow, and the bufferbloat test shows whether calls will stutter when the connection is busy.

Questions

What is the best internet provider in Lake Oswego, OR?

For most homes, fiber from Quantum Fiber if it reaches your street: up to 2 Gbps down and 1 Gbps up, with steady lag. It covers 31% of homes, so check your address first.

Is fiber internet available in Lake Oswego, OR?

Yes. CenturyLink, Quantum Fiber, Xfinity and Ziply Fiber report fiber in Lake Oswego, reaching at least 31% of homes.

What is the fastest internet in Lake Oswego, OR?

Ziply Fiber advertises up to 6 Gbps down and 6 Gbps up (fiber), offered to 10% of homes.

How many internet providers are in Lake Oswego, OR?

8: 4 wired (fiber, cable, or DSL) and 4 5G or fixed wireless, plus satellite from Starlink, Viasat, and HughesNet, which reaches almost every home.
