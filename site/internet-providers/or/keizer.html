Upload speeds in Keizer

Cable plans here top out at 250 Mbps up, against 1 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

5G home and fixed wireless

AT&T, Alyrica, Hunter Communications Inc, T-Mobile and Verizon offer wireless home internet in Keizer. It needs no installer and is often cheaper, but speed depends on the signal where you live and on how busy the tower is in the evening.

Before you switch

If your internet is slow now, make sure the provider is the problem. The free WiFi or ISP Mac app times your router and the internet at the same moment and tells you which side is slow, and the bufferbloat test shows whether calls will stutter when the connection is busy.

Questions

What is the best internet provider in Keizer, OR?

With little fiber reported yet, Xfinity (cable, up to 2 Gbps) covers the most homes. Compare upload speeds before you choose.

Is fiber internet available in Keizer, OR?

Barely: Alyrica, CenturyLink, Quantum Fiber and Xfinity report fiber to 1% of homes in Keizer. Check your address to see if yours is one.

What is the fastest internet in Keizer, OR?

Xfinity advertises up to 2 Gbps down and 250 Mbps up (cable), offered to 100% of homes.

How many internet providers are in Keizer, OR?

9: 5 wired (fiber, cable, or DSL) and 5 5G or fixed wireless, plus satellite from Starlink, Viasat, and HughesNet, which reaches almost every home.

Know whether it’s the provider before you switch.
