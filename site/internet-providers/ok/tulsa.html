Internet providers in Tulsa, OK

Tulsa has 12 home internet providers in the FCC's latest data, 7 of them wired. Fiber reaches at least 63% of homes, about the same as Oklahoma as a whole (67%), led by AT&T at up to 5 Gbps. Cox Communications cable covers 91% of homes, with uploads up to 500 Mbps.

Upload speeds in Tulsa

Cable plans here top out at 500 Mbps up, against 5.12 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

5G home and fixed wireless

AT&T, AirLink Internet Services, T-Mobile, The Junction Internet, Verizon and Wake Wireless offer wireless home internet in Tulsa. It needs no installer and is often cheaper, but speed depends on the signal where you live and on how busy the tower is in the evening.

Before you switch

If your internet is slow now, make sure the provider is the problem. The free WiFi or ISP Mac app times your router and the internet at the same moment and tells you which side is slow, and the bufferbloat test shows whether calls will stutter when the connection is busy.

Questions

What is the best internet provider in Tulsa, OK?

For most homes, fiber from AT&T if it reaches your street: up to 5 Gbps down and 5 Gbps up, with steady lag. It covers 63% of homes, so check your address first.

Is fiber internet available in Tulsa, OK?

Yes. AT&T, BTC Broadband, Bixby Telephone Co, Cox Communications, Metronet Holdings, Valor Telecommunications of Texas, LP and Windstream Communications, LLC. report fiber in Tulsa, reaching at least 63% of homes.

What is the fastest internet in Tulsa, OK?

AT&T advertises up to 5 Gbps down and 5 Gbps up (fiber), offered to 63% of homes.

How many internet providers are in Tulsa, OK?

12: 7 wired (fiber, cable, or DSL) and 6 5G or fixed wireless, plus satellite from Starlink, Viasat, and HughesNet, which reaches almost every home.

Know whether it’s the provider before you switch.

WiFi or ISP is a free Mac menu bar app. It times your router and the internet at the same moment, says which side is slow, and keeps a log you can send your ISP.
