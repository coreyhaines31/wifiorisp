Internet providers in Columbia, MO

Columbia has 13 home internet providers in the FCC's latest data, 7 of them wired. Fiber reaches at least 85% of homes, more than Missouri as a whole (66%), led by Socket Telecom LLC at up to 2.5 Gbps. Mediacom Xtream cable covers 91% of homes, with uploads up to 1 Gbps.

Upload speeds in Columbia

Cable plans here top out at 1 Gbps up, against 10 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

5G home and fixed wireless

AT&T, Air Link Rural Broadband LLC, Brightspeed, Stimulus Technologies, T-Mobile, Verizon and Wisper ISP, LLC offer wireless home internet in Columbia. It needs no installer and is often cheaper, but speed depends on the signal where you live and on how busy the tower is in the evening.

Before you switch

If your internet is slow now, make sure the provider is the problem. The free WiFi or ISP Mac app times your router and the internet at the same moment and tells you which side is slow, and the bufferbloat test shows whether calls will stutter when the connection is busy.

Questions

What is the best internet provider in Columbia, MO?

For most homes, fiber from Socket Telecom LLC if it reaches your street: up to 2.5 Gbps down and 2.5 Gbps up, with steady lag. It covers 85% of homes, so check your address first.

Is fiber internet available in Columbia, MO?

Yes. Brightspeed, Callabyte, Chariton Valley Communication Corporation, Juvilex Communications, Mediacom Xtream, Socket Telecom LLC and Spectrum report fiber in Columbia, reaching at least 85% of homes.

What is the fastest internet in Columbia, MO?

Socket Telecom LLC advertises up to 2.5 Gbps down and 2.5 Gbps up (fiber), offered to 85% of homes.

How many internet providers are in Columbia, MO?

13: 7 wired (fiber, cable, or DSL) and 7 5G or fixed wireless, plus satellite from Starlink, Viasat, and HughesNet, which reaches almost every home.

Know whether it’s the provider before you switch.

WiFi or ISP is a free Mac menu bar app. It times your router and the internet at the same moment, says which side is slow, and keeps a log you can send your ISP.
