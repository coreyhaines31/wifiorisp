Internet providers in Victoria, TX

Victoria has 10 home internet providers in the FCC's latest data, 4 of them wired. Fiber reaches at least 97% of homes, more than Texas as a whole (71%), led by Sparklight at up to 1 Gbps. Optimum cable covers 90% of homes, with uploads up to 35 Mbps.

Upload speeds in Victoria

Cable plans here top out at 35 Mbps up, against 5 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

5G home and fixed wireless

AT&T, Gtek Communications, Nextlink, Rise Broadband, T-Mobile, TISD Inc and Verizon offer wireless home internet in Victoria. It needs no installer and is often cheaper, but speed depends on the signal where you live and on how busy the tower is in the evening.

Before you switch

If your internet is slow now, make sure the provider is the problem. The free WiFi or ISP Mac app times your router and the internet at the same moment and tells you which side is slow, and the bufferbloat test shows whether calls will stutter when the connection is busy.

Questions

What is the best internet provider in Victoria, TX?

For most homes, fiber from Sparklight if it reaches your street: up to 1 Gbps down and 1 Gbps up, with steady lag. It covers 97% of homes, so check your address first.

Is fiber internet available in Victoria, TX?

Yes. AT&T, Infinium Broadband and Sparklight report fiber in Victoria, reaching at least 97% of homes.

What is the fastest internet in Victoria, TX?

AT&T advertises up to 5 Gbps down and 5 Gbps up (fiber), offered to 56% of homes.

How many internet providers are in Victoria, TX?

10: 4 wired (fiber, cable, or DSL) and 7 5G or fixed wireless, plus satellite from Starlink, Viasat, and HughesNet, which reaches almost every home.

Know whether it’s the provider before you switch.

WiFi or ISP is a free Mac menu bar app. It times your router and the internet at the same moment, says which side is slow, and keeps a log you can send your ISP.
