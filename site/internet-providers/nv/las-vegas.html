Internet providers in Las Vegas, NV

Las Vegas has 12 home internet providers in the FCC's latest data, 6 of them wired. Fiber reaches at least 31% of homes, less than Nevada as a whole (41%), led by Quantum Fiber at up to 8 Gbps. Cox Communications cable covers 82% of homes, with uploads up to 500 Mbps.

Upload speeds in Las Vegas

Cable plans here top out at 500 Mbps up, against 8 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

5G home and fixed wireless

AT&T, GeoLinks, Rise Broadband, T-Mobile, Verizon, WeLink Communications Inc and isp.net offer wireless home internet in Las Vegas. It needs no installer and is often cheaper, but speed depends on the signal where you live and on how busy the tower is in the evening.

Before you switch

If your internet is slow now, make sure the provider is the problem. The free WiFi or ISP Mac app times your router and the internet at the same moment and tells you which side is slow, and the bufferbloat test shows whether calls will stutter when the connection is busy.

Questions

What is the best internet provider in Las Vegas, NV?

For most homes, fiber from Quantum Fiber if it reaches your street: up to 8 Gbps down and 8 Gbps up, with steady lag. It covers 31% of homes, so check your address first.

Is fiber internet available in Las Vegas, NV?

Yes. AT&T, BAI Connect, CenturyLink, Cox Communications, Hotwire Communications and Quantum Fiber report fiber in Las Vegas, reaching at least 31% of homes.

What is the fastest internet in Las Vegas, NV?

Quantum Fiber advertises up to 8 Gbps down and 8 Gbps up (fiber), offered to 31% of homes.

How many internet providers are in Las Vegas, NV?

12: 6 wired (fiber, cable, or DSL) and 7 5G or fixed wireless, plus satellite from Starlink, Viasat, and HughesNet, which reaches almost every home.

Know whether it’s the provider before you switch.

WiFi or ISP is a free Mac menu bar app. It times your router and the internet at the same moment, says which side is slow, and keeps a log you can send your ISP.
