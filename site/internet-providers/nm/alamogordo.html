Internet providers in Alamogordo, NM

Alamogordo has 6 home internet providers in the FCC's latest data, 3 of them wired. Fiber reaches at least 16% of homes, less than New Mexico as a whole (30%), led by Tularosa Communications, Inc. at up to 1 Gbps. TDS Telecom cable covers 98% of homes, with uploads up to 20 Mbps.

Upload speeds in Alamogordo

Cable plans here top out at 20 Mbps up, against 1 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

5G home and fixed wireless

AT&T, T-Mobile, Tularosa Communications, Inc. and Verizon offer wireless home internet in Alamogordo. It needs no installer and is often cheaper, but speed depends on the signal where you live and on how busy the tower is in the evening.

Before you switch

If your internet is slow now, make sure the provider is the problem. The free WiFi or ISP Mac app times your router and the internet at the same moment and tells you which side is slow, and the bufferbloat test shows whether calls will stutter when the connection is busy.
