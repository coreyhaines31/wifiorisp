Upload speeds in Kennesaw

Cable plans here top out at 250 Mbps up, against 5 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

5G home and fixed wireless

AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon offer wireless home internet in Kennesaw. It needs no installer and is often cheaper, but speed depends on the signal where you live and on how busy the tower is in the evening.

Before you switch

If your internet is slow now, make sure the provider is the problem. The free WiFi or ISP Mac app times your router and the internet at the same moment and tells you which side is slow, and the bufferbloat test shows whether calls will stutter when the connection is busy.

Questions

What is the best internet provider in Kennesaw, GA?

For most homes, fiber from AT&T if it reaches your street: up to 5 Gbps down and 5 Gbps up, with steady lag. It covers 72% of homes, so check your address first.

Is fiber internet available in Kennesaw, GA?

Yes. AT&T, Google Fiber, Point Broadband Fiber Holding LLC and Xfinity report fiber in Kennesaw, reaching at least 72% of homes.

What is the fastest internet in Kennesaw, GA?

AT&T advertises up to 5 Gbps down and 5 Gbps up (fiber), offered to 72% of homes.

How many internet providers are in Kennesaw, GA?

6: 4 wired (fiber, cable, or DSL) and 3 5G or fixed wireless, plus satellite from Starlink, Viasat, and HughesNet, which reaches almost every home.

Know whether it’s the provider before you switch.
