Internet providers in Trujillo Alto, PR

Trujillo Alto has 14 home internet providers in the FCC's latest data, 4 of them wired. Fiber reaches at least 27% of homes, less than Puerto Rico as a whole (60%), led by Claro at up to 1 Gbps. Liberty cable covers 95% of homes, with uploads up to 30 Mbps.

Upload speeds in Trujillo Alto

Cable plans here top out at 30 Mbps up, against 4 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

5G home and fixed wireless

Aeronet Wireless Broadband LLC, Bluenet Communications LLC, COSOTNET, DM Wireless LLC, IP Solutions, InNet Connections, Natural G.C. INC, Neptuno Media, T-Mobile, Urban Networks Inc and VPNet offer wireless home internet in Trujillo Alto. It needs no installer and is often cheaper, but speed depends on the signal where you live and on how busy the tower is in the evening.

Before you switch

Questions

What is the best internet provider in Trujillo Alto, PR?

For most homes, fiber from Claro if it reaches your street: up to 1 Gbps down and 500 Mbps up, with steady lag. It covers 27% of homes, so check your address first.

Is fiber internet available in Trujillo Alto, PR?

Yes. Aeronet Wireless Broadband LLC, Claro, Liberty and Optico Fiber report fiber in Trujillo Alto, reaching at least 27% of homes.

What is the fastest internet in Trujillo Alto, PR?

Optico Fiber advertises up to 4 Gbps down and 4 Gbps up (fiber), offered to 19% of homes.

How many internet providers are in Trujillo Alto, PR?

14: 4 wired (fiber, cable, or DSL) and 11 5G or fixed wireless, plus satellite from Starlink, Viasat, and HughesNet, which reaches almost every home.

Know whether it’s the provider before you switch.

WiFi or ISP is a free Mac menu bar app. It times your router and the internet at the same moment, says which side is slow, and keeps a log you can send your ISP.
