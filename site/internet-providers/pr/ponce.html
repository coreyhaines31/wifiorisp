Internet providers in Ponce, PR

Ponce has 15 home internet providers in the FCC's latest data, 5 of them wired. Fiber reaches at least 37% of homes, less than Puerto Rico as a whole (60%), led by Claro at up to 1 Gbps. Liberty cable covers 97% of homes, with uploads up to 30 Mbps.

Upload speeds in Ponce

Cable plans here top out at 30 Mbps up, against 1 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

5G home and fixed wireless

Aeronet Wireless Broadband LLC, DM Wireless LLC, FamilyNet INC, Fuse Telecom LLC, GoNetworkPR, Neptuno Media, OSNET Wireless, Spectra Networks, T-Mobile, Urban Networks Inc, VPNet and XAIRNET CORP offer wireless home internet in Ponce. It needs no installer and is often cheaper, but speed depends on the signal where you live and on how busy the tower is in the evening.

Before you switch

If your internet is slow now, make sure the provider is the problem. The free WiFi or ISP Mac app times your router and the internet at the same moment and tells you which side is slow, and the bufferbloat test shows whether calls will stutter when the connection is busy.

Questions

What is the best internet provider in Ponce, PR?

For most homes, fiber from Claro if it reaches your street: up to 1 Gbps down and 500 Mbps up, with steady lag. It covers 37% of homes, so check your address first.

Is fiber internet available in Ponce, PR?

Yes. Aeronet Wireless Broadband LLC, Claro, Liberty, VPNet and Worldnet Telecommunications report fiber in Ponce, reaching at least 37% of homes.

What is the fastest internet in Ponce, PR?

Claro advertises up to 1 Gbps down and 500 Mbps up (fiber), offered to 37% of homes.

How many internet providers are in Ponce, PR?

15: 5 wired (fiber, cable, or DSL) and 12 5G or fixed wireless, plus satellite from Starlink, Viasat, and HughesNet, which reaches almost every home.

Know whether it’s the provider before you switch.

WiFi or ISP is a free Mac menu bar app. It times your router and the internet at the same moment, says which side is slow, and keeps a log you can send your ISP.
