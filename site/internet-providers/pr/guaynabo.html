Internet providers in Guaynabo, PR

Guaynabo has 19 home internet providers in the FCC's latest data, 9 of them wired. Fiber reaches at least 42% of homes, less than Puerto Rico as a whole (60%), led by Liberty at up to 1 Gbps. Liberty cable covers 95% of homes, with uploads up to 30 Mbps.

Upload speeds in Guaynabo

Cable plans here top out at 30 Mbps up, against 4 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

Before you switch

If your internet is slow now, make sure the provider is the problem. The free WiFi or ISP Mac app times your router and the internet at the same moment and tells you which side is slow, and the bufferbloat test shows whether calls will stutter when the connection is busy.

Questions

What is the best internet provider in Guaynabo, PR?

For most homes, fiber from Liberty if it reaches your street: up to 1 Gbps down and 1 Gbps up, with steady lag. It covers 42% of homes, so check your address first.

Is fiber internet available in Guaynabo, PR?

Yes. Aeronet Wireless Broadband LLC, Claro, FiberX, Liberty, Natural G.C. INC, Neptuno Media, Optico Fiber, Urban Networks Inc and Worldnet Telecommunications report fiber in Guaynabo, reaching at least 42% of homes.

What is the fastest internet in Guaynabo, PR?

Optico Fiber advertises up to 4 Gbps down and 4 Gbps up (fiber), offered to 38% of homes.

How many internet providers are in Guaynabo, PR?

19: 9 wired (fiber, cable, or DSL) and 15 5G or fixed wireless, plus satellite from Starlink, Viasat, and HughesNet, which reaches almost every home.

Know whether it’s the provider before you switch.
