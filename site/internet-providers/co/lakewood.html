Upload speeds in Lakewood

Cable plans here top out at 2 Gbps up, against 8 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

5G home and fixed wireless

AERUX BROADBAND, AT&T, LiveWireNet, Neteo Inc, Neteo Inc., Rise Broadband, Starry Inc, T-Mobile, Verizon, Visionary Broadband, Webpass, Inc. and WiFiHood offer wireless home internet in Lakewood. Before you switch

If your internet is slow now, make sure the provider is the problem. The free WiFi or ISP Mac app times your router and the internet at the same moment and tells you which side is slow, and the bufferbloat test shows whether calls will stutter when the connection is busy.

Questions

What is the best internet provider in Lakewood, CO?

For most homes, fiber from Quantum Fiber if it reaches your street: up to 8 Gbps down and 8 Gbps up, with steady lag. It covers 64% of homes, so check your address first.

Is fiber internet available in Lakewood, CO?

Yes. CenturyLink, Google Fiber, Quantum Fiber, Starry Inc and Xfinity report fiber in Lakewood, reaching at least 64% of homes.

What is the fastest internet in Lakewood, CO?

Quantum Fiber advertises up to 8 Gbps down and 8 Gbps up (fiber), offered to 64% of homes.

How many internet providers are in Lakewood, CO?

16: 5 wired (fiber, cable, or DSL) and 12 5G or fixed wireless, plus satellite from Starlink, Viasat, and HughesNet, which reaches almost every home.

Know whether it’s the provider before you switch.

WiFi or ISP is a free Mac menu bar app. It times your router and the internet at the same moment, says which side is slow, and keeps a log you can send your ISP.
