Internet providers in Davenport, IA

Davenport has 12 home internet providers in the FCC's latest data, 8 of them wired. Fiber reaches at least 93% of homes, more than Iowa as a whole (75%), led by Metronet Holdings at up to 5.12 Gbps. Mediacom Xtream cable covers 99% of homes, with uploads up to 1 Gbps.

Upload speeds in Davenport

Cable plans here top out at 1 Gbps up, against 8 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

5G home and fixed wireless

AT&T, CS Technologies, Inc., Central Scott Telephone Company, Inc., Nextlink, Night Owl Wireless, T-Mobile and Verizon offer wireless home internet in Davenport. It needs no installer and is often cheaper, but speed depends on the signal where you live and on how busy the tower is in the evening.

Before you switch

If your internet is slow now, make sure the provider is the problem. The free WiFi or ISP Mac app times your router and the internet at the same moment and tells you which side is slow, and the bufferbloat test shows whether calls will stutter when the connection is busy.

Questions

What is the best internet provider in Davenport, IA?

For most homes, fiber from Metronet Holdings if it reaches your street: up to 5.12 Gbps down and 5.12 Gbps up, with steady lag. It covers 93% of homes, so check your address first.

Is fiber internet available in Davenport, IA?

Yes. Central Scott Telephone Company, Inc., CenturyLink, Geneseo Communications Services Inc, Mediacom Xtream, Metronet Holdings, Night Owl Wireless and Quantum Fiber report fiber in Davenport, reaching at least 93% of homes.

What is the fastest internet in Davenport, IA?

Metronet Holdings advertises up to 5.12 Gbps down and 5.12 Gbps up (fiber), offered to 93% of homes.

How many internet providers are in Davenport, IA?

12: 8 wired (fiber, cable, or DSL) and 7 5G or fixed wireless, plus satellite from Starlink, Viasat, and HughesNet, which reaches almost every home.

Know whether it’s the provider before you switch.
