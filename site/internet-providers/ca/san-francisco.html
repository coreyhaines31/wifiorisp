Internet providers in San Francisco, CA

San Francisco has 14 home internet providers in the FCC's latest data, 9 of them wired. Fiber reaches at least 49% of homes, about the same as California as a whole (46%), led by Sonic Telecom, LLC at up to 10 Gbps. Xfinity cable covers 99% of homes, with uploads up to 2 Gbps.

Upload speeds in San Francisco

Cable plans here top out at 2 Gbps up, against 25 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

5G home and fixed wireless

AT&T, Etheric Networks, GeoLinks, Starry Inc, T-Mobile, Unwired Ltd, Verizon and Webpass, Inc. offer wireless home internet in San Francisco. It needs no installer and is often cheaper, but speed depends on the signal where you live and on how busy the tower is in the evening.

Before you switch

If your internet is slow now, make sure the provider is the problem. The free WiFi or ISP Mac app times your router and the internet at the same moment and tells you which side is slow, and the bufferbloat test shows whether calls will stutter when the connection is busy.

Questions

What is the best internet provider in San Francisco, CA?

For most homes, fiber from Sonic Telecom, LLC if it reaches your street: up to 10 Gbps down and 10 Gbps up, with steady lag. It covers 49% of homes, so check your address first.

Is fiber internet available in San Francisco, CA?

Yes. AT&T, Astound Broadband, BAI Connect, Etheric Networks, Race Communications, Sonic Telecom, LLC, Starry Inc and Xfinity report fiber in San Francisco, reaching at least 49% of homes.

What is the fastest internet in San Francisco, CA?

Sonic Telecom, LLC advertises up to 10 Gbps down and 10 Gbps up (fiber), offered to 49% of homes.

How many internet providers are in San Francisco, CA?

14: 9 wired (fiber, cable, or DSL) and 8 5G or fixed wireless, plus satellite from Starlink, Viasat, and HughesNet, which reaches almost every home.

Know whether it’s the provider before you switch.

WiFi or ISP is a free Mac menu bar app. It times your router and the internet at the same moment, says which side is slow, and keeps a log you can send your ISP.
