Herriman has 13 home internet providers in the FCC's latest data, 7 of them wired. Fiber reaches at least 32% of homes, less than Utah as a whole (73%), led by Quantum Fiber at up to 8 Gbps. Xfinity cable covers 92% of homes, with uploads up to 2 Gbps.

Upload speeds in Herriman

Cable plans here top out at 2 Gbps up, against 10 Gbps on fiber. If you work from home, back up to the cloud, or stream, the upload number matters more than the download. Our speed guide shows how much you need.

5G home and fixed wireless

AT&T, HiCountryNet, Kempler Internet, Rise Broadband, SenaWave, T-Mobile, Utah Broadband and Verizon offer wireless home internet in Herriman. It needs no installer and is often cheaper, but speed depends on the signal where you live and on how busy the tower is in the evening.

Before you switch

If your internet is slow now, make sure the provider is the problem. The free WiFi or ISP Mac app times your router and the internet at the same moment and tells you which side is slow, and the bufferbloat test shows whether calls will stutter when the connection is busy.

Questions

What is the best internet provider in Herriman, UT?

For most homes, fiber from Quantum Fiber if it reaches your street: up to 8 Gbps down and 8 Gbps up, with steady lag. It covers 32% of homes, so check your address first.

Is fiber internet available in Herriman, UT?

Yes. All West/Utah Inc., CenturyLink, HiCountryNet, Quantum Fiber, SenaWave, Utah Telecommunication Open Infrastructure Agency and Xfinity report fiber in Herriman, reaching at least 32% of homes.

What is the fastest internet in Herriman, UT?

Quantum Fiber advertises up to 8 Gbps down and 8 Gbps up (fiber), offered to 32% of homes.

How many internet providers are in Herriman, UT?

13: 7 wired (fiber, cable, or DSL) and 8 5G or fixed wireless, plus satellite from Starlink, Viasat, and HughesNet, which reaches almost every home.

Know whether it’s the provider before you switch.

WiFi or ISP is a free Mac menu bar app. It times your router and the internet at the same moment, says which side is slow, and keeps a log you can send your ISP.
